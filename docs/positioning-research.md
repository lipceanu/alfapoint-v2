## What this means

**Table stakes in 2026** (nearly everyone has these):
- A named AI service line (10 of 11 vendors)
- Discovery or product strategy
- Cloud/DevOps
- Maintenance
- At least two engagement models
- Case studies with metrics
- A Clutch profile
- ISO 27001

**Differentiators:**
- Agentic AI for clients
- AI-augmented delivery
- Legacy modernisation
- Technical audit or fractional CTO offers
- Published retention metrics
- For Saudi Arabia: a local office plus named government-platform integrations

**Naming:** no vendor uses "HR Services". The market says "Dedicated Teams", "Team/Staff Augmentation" or "Extended Teams".

**Saudi buyers expect:**
- PDPL compliance and in-region hosting
- NCA ECC controls (SAMA rules for fintech)
- ZATCA e-invoicing Phase 2
- Nafath, Mada and SADAD integrations
- Arabic-first RTL

## Changes made in v3

| v2 | v3 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (none) | **Product Discovery & MVP** | Discovery-led selling is standard; gives buyers a low-risk way in |
| Custom Software Development | **Custom Software Development** (kept; QA and support folded in) | Core offer |
| Data Science | **AI & Agentic Solutions** (data engineering folded in) | Every peer leads with AI; data science alone reads as dated |
| DevOps (sub-item) | **Cloud, DevOps & Modernisation** | Modernisation and FinOps are strong 2026 differentiators |
| HR Services | **Dedicated Teams & Staff Augmentation** | Industry naming; keeps the 10- and 20-day hiring promise |
| UI/UX Design | **UI/UX & Product Design** (+ Arabic/RTL) | Kept; RTL supports the GCC offer |
| (none) | **Saudi Arabia & GCC Delivery** | Uses the Riyadh presence, where there is real local demand |

Engagement models are now spelled out: Discovery sprint, Project delivery, Dedicated team and Staff augmentation.
